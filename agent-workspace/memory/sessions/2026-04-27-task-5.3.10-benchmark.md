## Fixture Choice

Used the verbatim architect §8.1 fixture: 4 tasks × 200ms per-task.

Rationale:
- 200ms per task puts serial at ~800ms and parallel at ~200ms (4× speedup, ~75% improvement).
- The 20% threshold has enormous slack even accounting for Windows scheduling jitter.
- No need to increase to 400ms — first run showed 75% improvement consistently.

No real subagent dispatches — pure synthetic setTimeout per architect §8.3 and Karpathy P2.
