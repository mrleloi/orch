**Bug 2 — Case γ two-key sidecar**: `tool_use_id` (toolu_*) at PreToolUse and `agent_id` (hex)
at SubagentStop are different ID spaces. Implemented three-phase correlation:
1. PreToolUse writes sidecar[toolu_*] entry
2. PostToolUse parses hex agentId from result text, writes sidecar[hex_id] → tool_use_id back-ref
3. SubagentStop looks up sidecar[hex_id], rewrites COMPLETED dispatch_id to toolu_*

Schema extended from 9 to 10 fields (added `tool_use_id`). Decision 023 lock preserved
(no field removed, existing readers ignore unknown fields).
